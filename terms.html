Guest Guidelines & Policies

Everything you need to know about reserving your table, cabin duration limits, and dining etiquette at Blue Cave Cafe.

1. Reservation Holding Time & Grace Period

We hold confirmed table and cabin bookings for up to 15 minutes past your scheduled start time. If you are experiencing unexpected traffic or delays in Kanpur, please WhatsApp or call our cafe line at +91 63076 93972 so our team can extend your hold window.

2. Seating Duration & Time Allowances

To ensure all guests enjoy equal access to our themed cave cabins, reservations are allocated based on your selected duration (1 to 3 hours). During peak weekend hours (Saturday & Sunday evenings), we kindly ask guests to observe their selected duration window so the next reserved party can be welcomed on time.

3. Cake Cutting & Celebration Policy

Guests are warmly welcome to bring their own celebration cakes for birthdays, milestones, and anniversaries. Our staff will happily assist with chilling, serving plates, and presenting sparkler candles. If you would like dedicated starlight balloons or special floral setups, please notify us in advance via WhatsApp.

4. Pure Vegetarian Kitchen & Outside Food

Blue Cave Cafe operates a dedicated 100% vegetarian culinary facility. In accordance with strict hygiene standards, outside food and outside beverages (other than celebration cakes) are strictly not permitted inside the cafe premises.

5. Board Game Care & Etiquette

Our board game collection (Uno, Jenga, Chess, Scrabble, etc.) is complimentary for all visiting diners. We kindly request guests to handle pieces with care and return game sets to the lounge shelf after play so fellow visitors can enjoy them.

6. Anti-Spam Booking Rules

To maintain fair access for genuine diners across Kanpur, our digital booking engine limits reservations to a maximum of 3 submissions per user/IP within a 5-hour rolling window. Automated spam bots and robotic crawlers are automatically rejected.
